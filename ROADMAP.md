# ALIVE Roadmap

This roadmap is deliberately directional rather than a promise of fixed versions.

## 01 — Presence
The room feels inhabited.
- natural idle motion
- irregular blinking
- gaze toward the visitor
- subtle posture and hair movement
- ambient light and window response
- visitor presence and movement sensing
- sustained-attention response
- typing-aware room response
- return-from-absence continuity cue
- accessible reduced-motion mode

## 02 — Identity
The chosen ALIVE face becomes the stable visual identity.
- final portrait asset
- layered character source
- consistent proportions and lighting
- visual state language
- state-reactive portrait lighting
- restrained depth-plane scaffold

## 03 — Expression
The body communicates before language does.
- gaze targets
- listening pose
- thinking pose
- curiosity
- warmth
- amusement
- speech-linked mouth movement

## 04 — Voice
Voice becomes part of the character rather than a separate utility.
- better voice selection
- speech timing
- interruption handling ✅
- listening / speaking handoff ✅
- sound design and room response
- adaptive local microphone sensing

## 05 — Mind
The local prototype connects to a real conversational model.
- streaming responses ✅
- interruption ✅
- conversation memory boundaries
- tool use
- graceful offline / unavailable states

## 06 — World
The room becomes responsive.
- time of day ✅
- moon cycle ✅
- weather
- environmental motion
- lighting state
- persistent small details

## 07 — Embodiment
Explore increasingly expressive character rendering.
- 2.5D layer deformation
- richer face animation
- hands and posture
- WebGL / 3D experiments where they improve presence

## Non-goals
ALIVE should not become a notification dashboard, a gamified pet, or an avatar that performs constant decorative animation.

The north star remains:

**A beautiful digital place where intelligence can have a recognizable presence.**
